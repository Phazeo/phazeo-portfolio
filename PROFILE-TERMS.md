# Profile terms

These terms apply to everyone who publishes a profile in this repository.

## What is public

Everything you put in this repository is public: source, assets, commit history, forks, and mirrors. Assume it can be copied and indexed forever. Do not put anything here that must stay private.

By opening a profile PR you confirm that **everything on your profile may be published** and that you are responsible for its accuracy and for having the right to publish it (including photos and resume PDFs).

## Never include

- Phone numbers
- Home or postal addresses
- Dates of birth (or equivalent phrases)

Email addresses and professional social links may appear when you choose to publish them. Prefer contact forms or “request a call” flows over listing a phone number.

## Content ownership and licence

- **Code** in this repository (the app shell, shared scripts, and profile UI code) is licensed under the MIT License in `LICENSE`.
- **Profile content** (your biography, experience text, project write-ups, photos you add as your portfolio material, and similar personal portfolio content) remains yours. It is **not** licensed for reuse under MIT. Others must not copy your profile content into their own portfolios or products without your permission.

## Invite-only

Profiles are invite-only. An approved GitHub account owns each slug (`owner` in `meta.ts`, or `meta.owner` in `profile.json` for data-first guests). Pull requests that change a profile must come from that owner (or the repository owner / CI bot), and still need the repository owner’s approval to merge.

## Leaving

On your last day with the team, your profile is set **unlisted** (direct URL may still work). After **30 days** it is removed from the tree unless you ask to stay as alumni. Git history will still contain older copies; request a removal if you need content taken down from the default branch.

## Requesting removal

Open a GitHub issue on this repository, or use the contact method shown on the live portfolio site. Do not put a personal email address in this file.
